NetThings Privacy Policy

NetThings stores network activity on your Mac, including app and process identifiers, transfer totals, connection times, addresses, ports and available hostnames. When supplied by macOS, a URL origin and path can also be saved. The monitor does not record packet contents. Settings controls how long detailed history is kept. Aggregated statistics may outlast the details.

Online services

To identify a destination’s country and network operator, NetThings sends eligible public destination IP addresses to IPinfo. Local lookup uses DB-IP data. Checking or downloading database updates contacts DB-IP. Public address lookup contacts ipify and can be disabled. Connectivity checks contact the configured HTTPS address and can also be disabled. These services can see the source IP of their requests. Apple processes trial and purchase transactions through the App Store.

隐私政策

NetThings 在你的 Mac 上保存 App 和进程标识、流量、连接时间、地址、端口和可用的域名。系统提供时也可保存网址的来源和路径。监控不会记录数据包正文。明细按设置中的期限保留，汇总统计可保留更久。

为显示远端国家和网络运营商，NetThings 会将符合条件的公网目标 IP 发给 IPinfo。本地查询使用 DB-IP 数据，检查和下载更新时会访问 DB-IP。查询本机公网地址会访问 ipify，连接检测会访问设置中指定的 HTTPS 地址，两项均可关闭。服务方可见请求来源 IP。试用和购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。
