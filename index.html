NetThings Support

For help with NetThings for Mac, email jazziii0608@gmail.com. Include your macOS version, NetThings version and what happened. You can preview a diagnostic report in the Help menu and choose whether to send it.

Monitoring

To see usage by app, approve the NetThings network extension and content filter when prompted by macOS. Open NetThings settings to check setup status.

Purchases

The 30-day trial ends without an automatic charge. To recover a previous unlock, choose Restore Purchases using the same App Store account.
